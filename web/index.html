How this fits together

    
The firmware is the playground app (apps/playground): the rfprobe bench shell plus a waveform buffer. The page talks to its shell over the C5's built-in USB-Serial-JTAG with Web Serial; the same cable flashes it. Two boards can be connected at once (A and B), so one transmits while the other captures.

    
Transmit engines

    
    
      
  • DAC 80 / 40 MS/s: the modem's DAC engine loops up to 16383 words out of its SRAM bank with no CPU. Anything periodic: tones, combs, chirps, noise, PSK/QAM, FSK, OFDM, your own I/Q file. Continuous, a burst of N passes, or pulsed with the transmitter off between bursts.
    • 
      
  • Paced: the CPU feeds the TX DC register at up to 250 kS/s (x1 to x8 interpolation), 2400 to 5900 MHz. Narrowband signals with fine frequency resolution.
    • 
      
  • Carrier: a constant DAC value, a clean CW at the LO.
    • 
      
  • Paint: pictures in a waterfall, a row of tones at a time.
    • 
    

    
Levels

    
The C5's power steps have no effect: the level follows the samples times the DAC scale. The chain compresses above amplitude × scale of about 1000. Wide multicarrier signals compress earlier: back the amplitude off for OFDM and noise. The analog filters pass about ±10 MHz in the 20 MHz mode and ±18 MHz in the 40 MHz mode.

    
Receive

    
Burst captures run at 80 down to 4 MS/s, up to 16380 samples (10-bit I/Q). The gapless diagnostic-bus ring gives 4-bit I/Q at 40 MS/s decimated in the chip. A board cannot receive while its DAC engine plays; use a second board, or the carrier mode.

    
Two-board check

    
    
      
  1. Connect A and B, tune both to the same LO (Transmit tab for A, Receive tab for B).
    2. 
      
  2. A: CW tone at +1 MHz, DAC 40 MS/s, Transmit.
    3. 
      
  3. B: Burst capture at 40 MS/s, Run. The peak sits at +1 MHz plus the two crystals' offset.

Running it locally

    
west build -b xiao_esp32c5/esp32c5/hpcore esp-sdr-zephyr/apps/playground
esp-sdr-zephyr/web/tools/stage.py playground=build
python3 -m http.server -d esp-sdr-zephyr/web 8000   # then http://localhost:8000

    
Web Serial needs a secure page: https, or http on localhost.
